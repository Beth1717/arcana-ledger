Everything is stored on this device, without an account. Other players installing the same app get their own private data.

Offline installation

After deployment on HTTPS, open the app in Chrome on Android, then use Install app or Add to Home screen. Open it once while online to cache its files. Test with airplane mode before relying on it during a session.

Uninstalling the app, clearing browser storage, or switching devices can remove local data. Export backups regularly. Imports replace all currently stored characters after confirmation.
